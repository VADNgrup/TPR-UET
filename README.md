#  Summary of Cross-modality Matching/Retrieval published by members in the Department of Information Systems, VNU University of Engineering and Technology (Updating)
This repository contains code for research projects on Cross‑modality Matching/Retrieval, developed with the participation of members from the Department of Information Systems, VNU University of Engineering and Technology (UET‑IS).

*This repository is now maintained by students of ADNgroup — a special research‑student group in UET‑IS.*

*We welcome collaboration from anyone who is passionate about AI and related fields. Please contact our representative at ngo19012005@gmail.com. Thank you!*
